# Move readout for the search-free policy

**Question.** How should the policy read out its move decision? Three candidate structures: a single readout token classified over the 1,969-move vocabulary (the status quo), a factored readout predicting from-square, to-square and promotion as independent factors (65/64/5 classes), and a wide readout concatenating four readout tokens through an MLP onto the same vocabulary. The question matters beyond accuracy: the move identity's token cost sets the trace length budget for any future sequence-style supervision.

**Method.** One round, three arms, only the readout head varying, on top of the settled board representation (one token per piece, joint piece×square table, side-to-move canonicalization, metadata suffix tokens). Models were ~87M-parameter 12-layer transformers (head parameter counts 1.51M / 0.10M / 15.5M respectively; declared, not equalized) trained on nested subsets of Stockfish-annotated (depth 16) human-game positions (524k and 992k unique positions, two passes each, identical update schedules). The headline metric is per-move negative log-likelihood of the teacher's best move; for the factored arm this is exactly the sum of the three factor cross-entropies, so all arms are compared in the same unit (nats per move). Paired bootstrap intervals over 8,192 validation positions; a further 8,192 held-out positions (disjoint from everything used in training or selection) replicated the comparison. Internal-health panels accompanied every run.

**Evidence.**

- The single-token readout won decisively at both scales: factored trailed by +1.05 nats at 524k and +1.12 at 992k; the wide readout trailed by +0.32 and +0.23. All intervals are far from zero.
- Under the legal-move argmax metric the factored arm statistically tied the winner (−0.4/−0.9 points, intervals crossing zero) while the wide arm was clearly worst (−2.9/−3.3 points).
- The factored arm learned move geometry quickly (probability mass on structurally valid from/to/promotion combinations rose from 0.16 to 0.70) but still wasted ~30% of its mass on invalid combinations at convergence.
- The wide arm showed a markedly flattened input-side representation (layer-0 effective rank 121–154 versus 293–310 for the single-token arm), without any instability or dead pathways anywhere.
- The held-out replication matched the selection set almost exactly (e.g., factored − single = +1.10 nats held-out versus +1.12 selection), so there is no sign of selection overfitting.

**Conclusion.** The policy keeps a single readout token over the full move vocabulary. The win is decisive at both scales, not a tie resolved by simplicity. The two eliminated arms fail for different reasons, and both failures are structurally informative: the factored readout's independence assumption (from × to × promotion) cannot express the sharply multimodal conditional distribution of destinations given an origin square, so it blurs probabilities (NLL +1.1) while preserving the argmax; the wide readout's extra capacity bought nothing and coincided with flattened early-layer representations. A residual unknown: the wide arm's deficit narrowed with data scale (0.32 → 0.23), so its ranking at hero scale is not strictly excluded by this toy regime; the factored arm's independence deficit is structural and does not have such an escape hatch. Final appeal belongs to hero scale.
